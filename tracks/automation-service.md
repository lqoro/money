# Track B: AI automation service (secondary upside)

Niche to test first: bilingual clinics and dental practices serving Spanish-speaking patients (your Spanish + healthcare background is the edge).

Start with workflows that touch **no patient health data**: appointment reminders and confirmations, review requests and replies, missed-call text-back, Spanish/English content and social posts, lead follow-up. Do not put PHI into any tool without a HIPAA business associate agreement.

1. Weeks 1-2: write one clear offer, build a demo workflow (n8n or Make + Claude API) on the Mac Mini. No coding needed; build it with Claude.
2. Weeks 3-4: 2-3 discounted pilot clients for testimonials.
3. Weeks 5-10: convert to retainers ($300-1,000/mo plus setup fee).
4. Mac Mini: always-on, remote access (Tailscale), backups, alerts.

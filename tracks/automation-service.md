# Track B: AI automation service (secondary upside)

Source of the playbook: the Nate Herk 368-video recap. Those prices and results are his claims, not verified, and he also sells courses. Use it as a checklist, not a promise.

Bottom line from the recap: one small-business pain, one automation, one retainer. Skip faceless AI channels, AI trading bots, and scraping/cold email without checking the rules.

## Offer (fill in)
One sentence: "I help [bilingual clinics / dental offices] [stop losing patients who call after hours] with [missed-call text-back and appointment follow-up]."

Start with workflows that touch **no patient health data**: missed-call text-back, appointment reminders, review requests and replies, lead follow-up, Spanish/English content. Anything with PHI needs HIPAA coverage first.

## The ladder
1. **Hours**: one-on-one session helping a business owner get set up with AI (sell time, not a project).
2. **Paid audit**: find the one process costing them hours or money.
3. **First project**: fixed price tied to value. Claimed first builds: $1,200-$2,600.
4. **Retainer**: monthly maintenance/optimization after results.

## Getting the first client (warm first)
- [ ] Day 1: write the offer sentence and list 20 trusted contacts (family, friends, coworkers, clinics you know, QuikTrip regulars who own businesses)
- [ ] Days 2-3: 5-10 low-pressure conversations; ask where they lose hours, don't pitch
- [ ] 1-2 free or discounted pilots, in exchange for a testimonial and numbers
- [ ] Only then: cold outreach, using the pilot as proof

## What sells (pick ONE first)
Missed-call text-back / speed-to-lead, document processing, follow-up sequences, database reactivation, customer support, voice receptionist.

## Pricing and KPI
- Price on the client's value, not your hours. Fixed build fee + monthly maintenance retainer.
- Before you start, agree one KPI and a baseline (for example, appointments booked per week).
- Ignore the "$20K/month retainer" claims; not a beginner benchmark.

## Delivery checklist
- [ ] Client owns the accounts (their Google/n8n/phone number); you are a team member
- [ ] Scope and "done" written down
- [ ] Separate maintenance retainer that excludes new features
- [ ] HTTPS and signed webhooks, no secrets in URLs, minimal personal data
- [ ] Error alerts to your phone; test with dummy data

## Build approach (no coding)
Describe the workflow to Claude, approve its plan, test it. Run on the Mac Mini (always-on, Tailscale for remote access, backups).

## 205. AI Agents Are Overused. Here's What to Build Instead
- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4OOS96i2gfI | **Length:** 11 min | **AI-relevant:** yes
- **Teaches:**
  - A four-layer "AI systems pyramid": custom GPTs/projects, plain automation (no AI), AI workflows, then AI agents. Complexity, cost and maintenance rise going up.
  - Decision test: must a human be in the loop every time (then custom GPT)? Is it pure rule logic (then workflow)? Is the step order fixed (then AI workflow)? Only open-ended, autonomous goals need an agent.
  - Start with the simplest fix. Nate estimates about half of business automations need no AI at all.
  - Agents can be made more predictable by wiring their tools to fixed workflows.
  - Solve the problem first; sometimes the answer is an existing SaaS product.
- **Tools / prices / numbers:** n8n (examples). Nate's rough estimate: about 50% of automations need no AI. No prices.
- **Skills shown:** workflow vs. agent design, problem scoping, n8n
- **Money angle:** Sell quick-win automations that keep client costs low; lead with the simplest solution. No prices stated.

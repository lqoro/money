## 110. The AI Offer You Can Sell Tomorrow Morning
- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pi-m8R068r4 | **Length:** 27 min | **AI-relevant:** yes
- **Teaches:**
  - Start by selling hours, not projects. The first offer is a one-on-one session helping a business owner set up their own AI operating system or get comfortable with Claude Code.
  - Use the "ladder": hours (rung 0), paid audit (rung 1), first project (rung 2), retainer (rung 3). Earn each rung from the one below.
  - Each session works as a mini sales call, a paid discovery session, and the first step in the relationship. Keep the owner's overwhelm low and don't make them feel behind.
  - Pitch "an operating system, not an AI tutorial." The business owner's own expertise is what makes the system work.
  - Seven-step plan to land first clients: teach friends, then text business owners you know, join communities and help, post case studies, build a portfolio, convert trust into projects, then approach local businesses.
- **Tools / prices / numbers:**
  - Rung prices as Nate gives them: hours $100 to $500 per session; paid audit $500 to $2,500; first project $2,500 to $10,000; retainer $3,000 to $10,000 per month.
  - He suggests starting lower, around $50 or $99 an hour, to get reps.
  - IBM 2026 CEO study as cited by Nate: 25% of employees use AI regularly; 85% of CEOs say employees have the skills.
  - Nate's claims: he built an agency to over $100,000 a month and exited it; his community has about 375,000 members.
- **Skills shown:** AI consulting and onboarding, client discovery, Claude Code setup, pricing and packaging, referral-based sales
- **Money angle:** Sell one-on-one AI OS setup sessions first, then audits, projects, and retainers.

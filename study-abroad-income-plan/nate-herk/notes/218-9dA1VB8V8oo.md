## 218. How to Sign Your First AI Automation Client (Without Starting an Agency)
- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9dA1VB8V8oo | **Length:** 15 min | **AI-relevant:** yes
- **Teaches:**
  - Skip the agency. Start as a freelancer, then consultant, then agency. Pick one niche where people lose hours to repetitive admin, and write one sentence naming who you help and what problem they have.
  - Build the offer with four Rs: result (a specific time or cost saving), roadmap (how you deliver it), risk reversal (free build for a testimonial, money-back, or pay-if-satisfied), and review (log objections, then fold them into the pitch).
  - Start with warm contacts (email, social, phone), not cold outreach. Use a human opener and the ACA framework: acknowledge, compliment, ask.
  - Discovery call (15 to 30 minutes, listen and probe), then a follow-up with a simple proposal. Overdeliver for the first one or two clients, track hours saved or errors cut, and collect a testimonial.
  - His "golden ratio": 60% traditional automation, 30% AI-assisted, 10% human approval.
- **Tools / prices / numbers:**
  - First paid client: $1,200 (Nate's claim).
  - Outreach goal he suggests: 50 messages a day for two weeks, then review.
  - Example result pitch (illustrative, not a measured result): onboarding that drops from 5 hours to 30 minutes.
  - Research helpers he names: Perplexity Deep Research, ChatGPT.
- **Skills shown:** niche research, offer design, sales outreach, discovery calls, client delivery, documenting results
- **Money angle:** Freelance AI automation for small businesses, sold on outcomes (time or money saved). Start with one or two free builds for testimonials, then charge. Price points: none stated beyond his $1,200 first-client claim. Start by listing 50 warm contacts and messaging them.

## 359. Build a No-Code AI Chatbot (Step-by-Step Tutorial)
- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B15e_9_fCyE | **Length:** 19 min | **AI-relevant:** yes
- **Teaches:**
  - Build a website chatbot in a no-code builder: crawl a site URL and upload an FAQ PDF into the knowledge base.
  - Chain blocks in a flow: send a message, capture a form (name, email) into variables, capture the visitor's question, generate an AI answer from the knowledge base.
  - Loop back after each answer with yes/no buttons so the chat continues until the visitor says no, then end with a personalized goodbye using a stored variable.
  - Customize branding (colors, logo, removing the platform badge), review conversations, and fix answers that were off.
  - Embed the bot on a site by pasting an HTML snippet (inline widget) into the page body after publishing.
- **Tools / prices / numbers:**
  - Chatling (no-code chatbot builder; free tier mentioned as "for free", no price stated)
  - AI model not named in the video; temperature kept near 0 (his setting)
  - Demo knowledge: one website page plus one FAQ PDF
- **Skills shown:** knowledge base setup, conversation flow design, variables, branching logic, website embedding, conversation review and tuning
- **Money angle:** Sell chatbot setup to small businesses for their websites to answer FAQs and capture leads; add a lead-capture form that writes to a sheet. The video says lead-gen bots can store contact details but gives no pricing.

## 2. I Tried 100+ AI Tools. These 7 Are the Best
- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hgLI491ajG0 | **Length:** 10 min | **AI-relevant:** yes
- **Teaches:**
  - Nate's list of seven AI tools he uses daily to run his business, chosen from over 100 he tested.
  - Claude Code or Codex as a local agent that reads and writes files; he uses it as a "second brain" and to replace paid apps by building custom ones.
  - OpenRouter as one account and one bill for many models, with fallbacks if a model fails and a separate key per automation for cost tracking.
  - Split work by cost: a cheap model sorts leads, a stronger model writes replies.
  - A VPS (always-on cloud computer) for hosting agents, and trigger.dev for deterministic TypeScript jobs that do not need an agent.
- **Tools / prices / numbers:**
  - Claude Code and Codex (both paid; he uses both weekly).
  - OpenRouter: over 500 models from more than 80 providers.
  - Hostinger VPS, with one-click templates (sponsored segment).
  - Grok Bot: scheduled and webhook bots on a shared cloud computer, with a phone app.
  - Glydo: voice dictation with a command mode and custom MCP tools (Nate is a partner).
  - key.ai: pay-per-use image and video models, no subscription.
  - trigger.dev: free plan includes $5 of credits per month (Nate's claim).
- **Skills shown:** AI agent setup, prompt-driven building, model routing and fallbacks, API key management, VPS hosting, scheduled automation, voice-driven workflow
- **Money angle:** Nate suggests businesses cut tools they pay for but use for one feature, and sell AI automations to clients (for example, team Q&A over SOPs with Claude Code, or deterministic automations on trigger.dev). Price points are not stated beyond the trigger.dev free tier.
- **Duet-relevant:** Grok Bot runs always-on bots in the cloud that act on schedules and webhooks and are controlled from a phone, which matches a persistent personal agent.
- **Promo:** Free AIOS kit (his "Herc Loop" framework, six skills) linked in the description; his free community and paid "Plus" community; Hostinger sponsorship (hostinger.com/natehertzvps, Black Friday sale); Glydo partnership.

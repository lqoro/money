## 312. 6 Months of Building AI Agents in 43 Minutes (without the hype)
- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QhujcQk8pyU | **Length:** 43 min | **AI-relevant:** yes
- **Teaches:**
  - Separate AI workflows (fixed steps with AI inside) from agents (the model picks its own steps). Most problems only need a workflow, which is easier to debug.
  - Wireframe before building: map the trigger, data flow, branches, AI decision points, and integrations.
  - Use AI only where it adds value, such as classifying free text. LLM calls cost money.
  - Give the agent a system prompt, short-term memory, and retrieval. Use SQL for structured data instead of vectors.
  - Prompt reactively: start bare, add one tool and one rule at a time, and scale one process fully before adding more.
- **Tools / prices / numbers:** n8n (built on LangChain); Excalidraw for wireframes. Nate's claim: his agency helps clients scale without adding headcount. Seven lessons; no prices.
- **Skills shown:** workflow architecture, scoping and wireframing, prompt design, RAG, evaluation and guardrails.
- **Money angle:** Nate's claim: he scopes client work with wireframes before quoting, since underpricing and missing integrations are common. No price points are given.

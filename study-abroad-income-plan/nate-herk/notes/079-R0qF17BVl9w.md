## 79. Fable 5 Just Built Me a Business With One Prompt
- **Link:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R0qF17BVl9w | **Length:** 12 min | **AI-relevant:** yes
- **Teaches:**
  - Put a long master prompt (mission, guardrails, phases, definition of done) in a file, then tell `/goal` to read it, because `/goal` has a short character limit
  - Orchestration pattern: Fable plans, delegates, and reviews, while Opus or Sonnet sub-agents do the research and building
  - Pipeline: pain hunt (research, then verify each quote), tournament judging, brand, build, videos, red team, packaging
  - Use a skeptic agent to try to kill the idea before building
  - Set a definition of done that a stranger can check from one recap HTML page
- **Tools / prices / numbers:**
  - Claude Fable 5 as orchestrator; Opus and Sonnet as workers
  - Run used about 500k Fable tokens; about 15% of his weekly Fable budget and 30-40% of his 5-hour limit on a $200/month plan (his estimates)
  - Research: 10 agents, 35 raw problems merged into 18 candidates, all 18 verified; five judges scored them
  - Product pricing in the sample: $19 flat per approved response, no success fee
  - Took about 3-4 hours (his estimate)
- **Skills shown:** `/goal` prompting, multi-agent orchestration, adversarial verification, market research, brand and logo design, launch-video creation, business planning
- **Money angle:** The sample is "Counter Brief," a chargeback-dispute tool for Shopify stores with 3+ disputes a month, priced at $19 per approved response with no success fee. Nate says the business was built locally and not published. He also says the idea needs stress-testing before building.

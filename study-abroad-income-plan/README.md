## What this is built from
- **368 videos, about 156 hours**, from youtube.com/@nateherk as listed on 2026-10-25. Transcripts were read (YouTube captions) and condensed into one note per video by Haiku agents, then synthesized into the files above.
- Raw transcripts are **not** stored here; the notes paraphrase the videos.
- Spot-check: 12 key numbers (prices and claims) matched the transcripts. Notes were not otherwise audited.

## How to read it
- `Vnnn` = video number in the index.
- **Dollar amounts are claims from the videos** unless marked *inference*. Nate promotes a free school community, a paid "Plus" community, courses and sponsors; treat income stories as marketing.
- Things in [CLAUDE-DUET-READY.md](CLAUDE-DUET-READY.md) about Claude duet are assumptions to check once it is released.
- Model names and product features in the videos change quickly; check current details.

## Known gaps
- V167 (a 10+ hour course) has a note based on parts of the transcript.
- A few notes carry a caption-language caveat (the transcript was machine-translated); tool names there may be wrong.
- Visa, tax and legal rules depend on your country and school. The risks file lists what to ask, it does not answer it.

# Fast Money: first real dollars by Sunday Nov 1, 2026

Researched Mon Oct 26, 2026. Ranking is by one test: is the money likely to be in your account or hand by Sunday night, not just "earned". All dollar figures below are either a cited platform figure, a cited third-party figure, or labeled as my estimate.

## TL;DR (Mon to Sun plan)

1. **Mon:** List 20 to 50 warm contacts (family, friends' parents, old bosses, club leaders, shops you go to) and 5 to 15 unused items. Claude writes the outreach messages and the item listings tonight; post the items on Facebook Marketplace and campus groups.
2. **Tue to Wed:** Send messages offering one fixed-price fix to small businesses (Google Business Profile cleanup, one-page site, menu or flyer, review replies) and one to students and clubs (resume edit, club flyer). Offer the B2B lead sample (10 free leads, then a paid batch) to any warm contact who sells to businesses. Meet buyers for items.
3. **Thu to Fri:** Deliver the first jobs with Claude doing the drafting and building. Get paid on delivery by Zelle, cash, or Venmo (marked as goods and services). Ask each paying customer for one referral.
4. **Sat to Sun:** Finish deliveries, collect payment, sell the rest of the items. In spare time, apply to Prolific, Outlier, UserTesting, and set up Upwork for next week's cash; none of them is likely to pay out by Sunday.
5. Realistic target (my estimate, not a promise): $50 to $300 by Sunday from 1 to 3 paid jobs plus item sales. Zero is possible if nobody you message says yes, so send more messages, not better ones.

## 1. Quick services for local businesses (warm contacts and nearby shops)

**What to do.** Pick one fixed-price offer per business type so the message is easy to say yes to:
- Google Business Profile cleanup: correct hours, categories, description, photos, services list, and drafted replies to the last 10 to 20 reviews.
- One-page website (hours, menu or services, map, contact button) on a free or cheap host.
- Menu, flyer, or holiday-hours sign redesign (print-ready PDF). Late October timing fits Halloween, Thanksgiving, and holiday-hours signs.
- Review-reply drafts: you draft, the owner posts or approves.

**Where.** Your warm list first: family members who own or work at a business, friends' parents, past employers, the coffee shop or barber you already use. Then walk into 5 to 10 shops near campus with a printed before-and-after of one real example. Nate Herk's advice lines up with this: list warm contacts and message them before any cold outreach (video #151, "list 50 warm contacts"; video #180, a 7-day warm-first plan: day 1 list 20 trusted contacts, days 2 to 3 hold 5 to 10 low-pressure conversations). He usually says to do the first build free for a testimonial (#151, #297) and to start with a small paid session of $100 to $200 (#304, his figure). For this week, a middle path: show a free sample on their real listing (for example, three rewritten review replies or a draft description), then charge for the full job.

**Earnings this week.** My estimate: $25 to $150 per small job for a student with no portfolio, so $0 to $300 total from 1 to 3 jobs. Not market data. Limit is how many people you ask, not delivery time.

**Payout timing.** Same day if you ask for payment on delivery. Zelle has no Zelle fee (your bank sets limits and fees) ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/article/small-business/zelle-for-business)). Venmo: the user agreement says personal accounts must not be used for commercial transactions unless the payment is marked as goods and services or you use a business profile; goods and services payments carry a seller fee ([Venmo agreement summary](https://conductatlas.com/platform/venmo/venmo-user-agreement/personal-account-commercial-use-prohibition/), [PetaPixel 2021](https://petapixel.com/2021/06/28/venmo-will-let-personal-users-sell-goods-and-services-for-a-price)). Sources disagree on the current fee (1.9% plus $0.10 in most guides, 2.99% in one); check Venmo's fee page. Cash avoids both.

**How AI does the work.** Claude drafts the outreach messages, the profile description, service lists, review replies, and builds the one-page site in Claude Code. Claude or another image tool drafts flyer layouts; Muse can help with research and drafting. You must: have the conversation and close the sale, get the owner to add you as a manager on their Google Business Profile (Google lets owners invite managers, and managers can reply to reviews; each person needs their own Google account) ([Google help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3403100?hl=en-GB)), check every fact (hours, prices, allergens) with the owner, and get their OK before anything goes live.

**Risks and rules.** Never write fake reviews or offer anything in exchange for positive reviews: the FTC rule in effect since Oct 21, 2024 bans fake and AI-generated reviews and allows civil penalties ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)). Do not ask for the owner's password; use the manager invite. Agree the price and the exact deliverable in one text message before starting.

## 2. Sell unused items locally with AI-written listings

**What to do.** Gather 5 to 15 things you do not use (textbooks, electronics, furniture, clothes, gear). Photograph each. Have Claude write the title, description, condition notes, and a price check against similar listings you paste in.

**Where.** Facebook Marketplace (local pickup), campus buy-and-sell groups, Discord or GroupMe groups, OfferUp.

**Earnings.** Depends on what you own; no estimate is honest here.

**Payout timing.** Same day at pickup. Third-party guides say local pickup on Facebook Marketplace has no selling fee, while shipped orders pay about 10% ([Closo](https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/does-fb-marketplace-have-fees), [Vendoo calculator](https://vendoo.co/fbmp-fees-calculator)). These are not Meta's own docs.

**AI vs human.** AI writes listings and replies to buyer messages. You price, photograph, meet the buyer, and take payment.

**Risks.** Meet in a public place. Take cash or an instant payment before handing over the item. Ignore buyers who offer to overpay, send a check, or ask you to text a verification code; these are common scam patterns.

## 3. Campus gigs: resume edits, club flyers, peer tutoring

**What to do.** Three offers:
- Resume and LinkedIn rewrite for classmates before recruiting deadlines (Claude drafts, you review with them in a 20-minute call).
- Flyers, event posters, and social graphics for clubs and Greek life events.
- Peer tutoring in a subject you are strong in.

**Where.** Club group chats, class Discords, dorm groups, your career center's bulletin boards if allowed, friends who run clubs.

**Earnings.** My estimate: $0 to $150 this week. Not market data.

**Payout timing.** Same day by Venmo or Zelle. Platform tutoring is slower: Wyzant pays on the 1st and 15th for lessons submitted five days before, keeps 25%, and requires direct deposit ([Wyzant](https://support.wyzant.com/tutors/tutor-payments/tutor-payment-schedule-update-july-2025/), [fees](https://support.wyzant.com/tutors/tutor-payments/what-is-the-fee-structure-for-tutors-listed-on-wyzant/)). The Nov 1 payout would need lessons submitted by about Oct 27, and approval time is unverified, so do not count on Wyzant this week.

**AI vs human.** AI drafts resumes, flyer copy, layouts, and practice problems. You must do the live explaining when tutoring and keep the client's real experience accurate on resumes.

**Risks.** Academic integrity: tutor and explain, never do graded homework, essays, or take-home exams for someone. That breaks honor codes and can get both of you disciplined. Check your school's rules on selling services in dorms or campus spaces. Resumes must not invent experience.

## 4. B2B "10 free leads" sample, then a paid batch

**What to do.** For a warm contact who sells to businesses (a realtor, insurance agent, agency owner, contractor), build 10 free leads matching their ideal customer, then offer a one-off paid batch (for example 50 to 100 leads). This also tests the longer retainer business.

**Where.** Your warm list only this week. Nate Herk's video #186 example is a weekly list of dental practices sold as leads for website sellers (no price given), and #145 suggests "offer one free audit this week".

**Earnings.** My estimate: $0 to $200 for one batch this week. Not market data.

**Payout timing.** Same day if the buyer pays on delivery by Zelle or cash.

**AI vs human.** Claude Code scripts collect and clean public business data and draft a short note per lead. You must spot-check every lead (wrong emails ruin the sample), agree on criteria with the buyer, and confirm their outreach plan follows CAN-SPAM and any state rules.

**Risks.** Only use public business data, follow each source site's terms (many directories ban scraping), and do not sell personal consumer data. Realism: most warm contacts will not need leads; treat this as a bonus, not the main plan.

## 5. Prolific studies

**What to do.** Join and complete academic surveys and studies.

**Payout timing.** The best of any platform here: cashouts usually arrive within 24 hours, minimum $6, no fee to PayPal, once per 24 hours, and only once every 72 hours for the first 4 cashouts after you add or change your PayPal ([Prolific help](https://participant-help.prolific.com/en/articles/445067-cashing-out-with-paypal-faq-and-troubleshooting)). Study rewards are usually approved by the researcher first; approval timing I could not verify.

**Limit.** New sign-ups join a waitlist that is not first-come; invites can come in hours or months ([Prolific](https://www.prolific.com/blog/were-introducing-a-waitlist-for-new-participants)). One Reddit thread (Feb 2026, anecdotal) guessed 2 weeks to a month for most.

**AI vs human.** You must answer studies yourself. Using AI to answer surveys is a breach of Prolific's purpose and gets accounts removed. Sign up Monday; if you get in, it is a small bonus.

## 6. AI training and data annotation (Outlier, Alignerr, Mercor, DataAnnotation)

**Payout timing.**
- Outlier: third-party sources say pay goes out Tuesdays for work done the previous Tuesday to Monday ([aitrainer.work](https://aitrainer.work/guides/outlier-review), [aiworkfinder](https://aiworkfinder.com/blog/outlier-ai-review-2026-is-it-legit-pay-rates-projects-and-what-they-dont-tell-you/)). Work done this week would pay on Tue Nov 3, after Sunday. Waits for a first project are reported from days to about two months.
- DataAnnotation: approval reported at 1 to 4 weeks, each task has a 7-day approval window before you can withdraw ([Riseup Labs](https://riseuplabs.com/how-long-to-get-accepted-data-annotation/), [breakingeven](https://breakingeven.online/platforms/dataannotation)). Not possible by Sunday.
- Alignerr: reported 1 to 3 days to start, bi-weekly or weekly pay (sources conflict) ([aitrainer.work](https://aitrainer.work/compare/alignerr-vs-mercor), [Alignerr help](https://intercom.help/alignerr/en/articles/9554833-alignerr-onboarding)).
- Mercor: screening reported around three weeks ([aitrainer.work](https://aitrainer.work/compare/alignerr-vs-mercor)).

All of these are third-party reports, not official schedules.

**AI vs human.** These platforms pay for human judgment. Using AI to produce your answers is generally banned and gets accounts removed. You do the work yourself.

**Risks.** Job scams copy these brands. The FTC says never pay to get a job and to ignore unexpected job texts, WhatsApp, or Telegram messages ([FTC via NBC](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/national-international/beware-of-job-scams-how-to-protect-yourself/6330073/?amp=1)). Fake "DataAnnotation recruiters" have been reported ([BBB Scam Tracker](https://www.bbb.org/scamtracker/lookupscam/940438)). Apply only on the company's own site. Worth applying this week for later income, not for Sunday.

## 7. Paid user testing (UserTesting, dscout, Respondent)

**Payout timing.**
- UserTesting: its support site says PayPal payment usually 14 days after the test; blogs say 7 days ([search summary of support.usertesting.com](https://support.usertesting.com/hc/articles/11130947874323), page blocked my direct fetch, so not fully verified). Pay figures reported: $4 per 5-minute test, $10 per 20-minute test (third party) ([Household Money Saving](https://www.householdmoneysaving.com/usertesting-review/)).
- dscout: rewards generally within 10 business days of the mission closing; usability tests have a 7-day hold ([dscout participant help](https://participanthelp.dscout.com/getting-paid/payment-basics)).
- Respondent: 5 to 10 or 7 to 10 business days after the researcher confirms, now via Tremendous, minus a 5% fee (min $1) ([Respondent help](https://help.respondent.io/en/articles/12552519-getting-paid-on-respondent-timeline-currencies-how-to-redeem), [status](https://help.respondent.io/en/articles/5456523-what-is-the-status-of-my-incentive-payment)).

None realistically pays by Sunday. Sign up now; this is next-week and later money.

**AI vs human.** You do the tests yourself on camera or mic. AI can only help you set up profiles honestly. Lying in screeners breaks their terms.

## 8. Upwork micro-jobs

**Timing.** Profile review reported at 24 to 48 hours (older moderator posts and a guide) ([Upwork community](https://community.upwork.com/t5/New-to-Upwork/How-can-I-know-that-my-profile-is-approved/m-p/537546/highlight/true)). Fixed-price payments go through a 5-day security period after the client approves the milestone ([Upwork community staff](https://community.upwork.com/t5/Freelancers/how-long-to-get-paid-after-contract-ends/m-p/1263737)). Even a job approved on Tuesday would clear Sunday at the earliest, and you also need to win the job first. Connects cost $0.15 each per third-party sources ([GoLance](https://golance.com/blogs/upwork-fees-explained-2026)).

**What to do.** Set up the profile this week for later. Bid on small, fast jobs that match option 1 (Google profile fixes, landing pages, data cleanup).

**AI vs human.** AI drafts proposals and does the work. You must review everything, follow any client instruction about AI, and not misrepresent your skills.

## 9. Fiverr gigs

**Timing.** Most sources put the clearance period at 14 days for most sellers and 7 days for Top Rated sellers ([ConductAtlas ToS summary](https://conductatlas.com/platform/fiverr/fiverr-terms-of-service/payment-clearance-period-14-day-hold/), [WalletGrower 2026](https://walletgrower.com/blog/fiverr-review-2026)). Fiverr's help center mentions an early payout option for "eligible sellers" at a 1% fee ([Fiverr help](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/9234443621137), page blocked my direct fetch). I could not verify whether a brand-new seller is eligible. Plan on no Fiverr cash this week.

**AI rules.** Fiverr's AI guidelines say you must own the rights to AI-assisted work, disclose AI use when asked, honor a request for non-AI work, and that unmodified AI output does not meet quality standards ([Fiverr AI guidelines](https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/37333301560593)).

## 10. Digital templates or prompt packs (Gumroad, Etsy)

**Timing.** Gumroad pays weekly on Fridays; third-party sources disagree on new-seller holds and the minimum ($10 vs $100) ([Gumroad docs mirror](https://mintlify.com/antiwork/gumroad/payments/overview), [Latuos](https://latuos.com/gumroad-fees/)). Etsy says new sellers' funds are generally available 14 to 20 days after a sale and a reserve of up to 45 days can apply ([Etsy help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360002080688)). With no audience, first sales are unlikely this week anyway. Slow; skip for this goal.

## Taxes and paperwork (applies to all)

- If your net self-employment earnings for the year reach $400, you must file and pay self-employment tax (Schedule SE) ([IRS Taxpayer Advocate](https://taxpayeradvocate.irs.gov/get-help/paying-taxes/self-employment-taxes)). Keep a simple log of every payment and expense starting now.
- For 2026, PayPal, Venmo and similar apps must send a 1099-K only above $20,000 and 200 transactions ([TaxAct](https://blog.taxact.com/4-common-misconceptions-about-the-new-1099-k-reporting-threshold/), [CPA Practice Advisor](https://cpapracticeadvisor.com/2025/07/30/one-big-beautiful-bill-act-changes-1099-thresholds/165863/)). Income below that is still taxable. Some older pages still quote $600; that figure was set aside.
- If you are on a student visa (for example F-1), self-employment and off-campus work are restricted. Ask your international student office before taking any paid work. (General knowledge, not verified for this file.)

## Not recommended

- **Trading or crypto bots:** you have no capital to risk, results are not income, and losses are likely. Nate Herk's own trading-bot videos (#223, $10,000 of real cash per bot; #231) are experiments, not a first-income method.
- **Gambling and "matched betting":** not income, often breaks sportsbook terms, and has age and state limits.
- **MLM:** most participants lose money; you pay to join.
- **Faceless YouTube or TikTok channels:** monetization thresholds take months, not days.
- **Using AI to answer surveys, user tests, or annotation tasks:** breaks those platforms' rules and gets accounts banned.
- **Writing essays or doing homework for pay:** breaks academic integrity rules.
- **Fake reviews, review swaps, or paid positive reviews:** banned by the FTC rule.
- **Any "job" that asks you to pay a fee, deposit, or buy crypto to unlock earnings, or that recruits you over WhatsApp or Telegram:** FTC-described task scam pattern.
- **Get-rich-quick courses or "AI side hustle" bundles:** you pay up front for advice you already have here.

# After $10/hr: scaling up

Do these in order. Each step needs the one before it working first.

| Step | Trigger to start | What changes | Rough ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Raise volume | Each ramp step in `MEMORY.md` runs clean (opt-outs < 1%, bounces < 3%) | Follow the `send_ramp` table: 20 → 40 → 80 → 150 → 200 emails a day across more domains and inboxes | ~25 clients |
| 2. Add-on revenue | Client has been on 4+ weeks | Outreach Drafts add-on, +$200/mo (3-email sequence per lead, humanized) | +50% revenue per client |
| 3. Raise the price | 10+ clients, churn < 10%/mo | New clients pay $500–600; keep the early clients on their old price | — |
| 4. Second niche | Niche 1 reply rate drops under 2% | Switch `current_niche` to the next ICP; reuse every lesson | — |
| 5. Split Muse into roles | ~25 clients, when one agent's runs start colliding | A Prospector agent, a Delivery agent and a Chief of Staff that routes between them (recap #310, #322: one job per agent) | ~50 clients |
| 6. Productize | A clear best niche with case studies | A higher tier: done-for-you outreach sending on the client's own inbox, $1,500+/mo (recap #304 service ladder) | $20k+/mo |

What not to do (from the recap): don't put the money into trading bots or faceless channels, don't scrape behind logins,
and don't remove the approval gate to "save time". One bad mass email can burn the domain and the business with it (recap #316).
